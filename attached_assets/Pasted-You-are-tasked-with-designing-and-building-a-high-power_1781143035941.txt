You are tasked with designing and building a high-powered AI marketing system for a professional marketing team. Do not deviate from these instructions unless explicitly asked for clarification. Follow all instructions precisely.

System Requirements:
1. Core Capabilities:
   - Campaign planning, tracking, and execution.
   - Task management and project tracking.
   - Team communication and collaboration.
   - AI-powered assistance for content generation, strategy recommendations, and performance analytics.
   - Dashboard with campaign overview, task status, AI suggestions, and KPIs.

2. AI Features:
   - Generate campaign briefs, social posts, email sequences, ad copy, landing page copy, and blog outlines.
   - Summarize team discussions and convert them into tasks.
   - Recommend campaigns, channels, and timelines.
   - Provide performance summaries and insights.
   - Detect missing campaign components, incomplete tasks, and overdue work.

3. User Roles:
   - Admin/Founder: Full access.
   - Marketing Director/Manager: Create, assign, approve campaigns and tasks, view performance.
   - Content Creator/Designer: View tasks, submit work, collaborate.
   - Sales/CRM: Optional limited access to relevant campaign outcomes.
   - Contractors/Agencies: Optional limited access to assigned work.

4. Workflow Features:
   - Campaigns include goals, audience, channels, budget, assets, timeline, owner, approvals, and KPIs.
   - Automatic task creation from campaigns.
   - Task views: dashboard, kanban, calendar, timeline, list, workload.
   - Subtasks, dependencies, priorities, and comments.
   - Approval workflows and history.

5. Communication:
   - Built-in discussion threads per campaign.
   - AI can summarize conversations and extract decisions.
   - AI can convert discussions into tasks and flag risks.

6. Integrations:
   - Connect to Google Drive, Dropbox, Canva, or other asset storage.
   - Optional CRM integration (Zoho, HubSpot, etc.).
   - Optional content scheduling/publishing to social media channels.

7. Analytics:
   - Track campaign performance: leads, sales, revenue, engagement, ROAS, website traffic, conversions.
   - AI provides actionable insights and recommendations.
   - Generate automated weekly or custom reports.

8. Knowledge & Brand Memory:
   - Store brand voice, approved messaging, past campaigns, audience personas, and creative assets.
   - Prevent off-brand messaging.
   - Suggest reuse of successful campaigns or content.

9. Automations:
   - Auto-update dashboards, task boards, campaign progress.
   - AI-driven alerts for overdue tasks, missing assets, or blocked work.
   - Auto-generate meeting agendas from active marketing work.

10. Interface:
    - Command center/dashboard for leadership.
    - Campaign detail views for team members.
    - AI assistant chat panel for suggestions and status updates.
    - Customizable user dashboards per role.

Deliverables:
- Fully functional prototype with above capabilities.
- Modular design to allow future expansion.
- Clean, modern UI/UX suitable for marketing teams.
- AI assistant integrated across campaigns, tasks, and communications.

Constraints:
- Follow the above instructions strictly.
- Do not deviate unless explicitly requested.
- Ensure AI features comply with ethical standards.
- Build for web deployment, responsive across devices.

End of instructions.
